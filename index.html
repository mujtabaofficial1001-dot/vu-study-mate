How to Install VU Study Mate

Since we are sharing this app directly through GitHub for students to use completely free, you must manually install it on your Android phone. Follow these quick steps:

01
Download the APK file

Click the prominent "Download App" button at the top to download the official secure `VU.STUDY.MATE.apk` directly to your phone.

02
Enable Unknown Sources

If prompted by your browser (Chrome/Edge), tap on "Settings" and toggle "Allow from this source" to allow sideloading installation.

03
Click Install & Enjoy

Open the downloaded file, click "Install", enter your program & semester profile details, and start exploring your study files offline!
