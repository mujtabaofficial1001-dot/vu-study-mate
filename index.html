+
Yes, VU StudyMate is 100% secure. It utilizes local sandbox paths (context.getExternalFilesDir) and only requires INTERNET permission to sync documents from Firebase storage. The APK has been audited via VirusTotal (SHA-256 verification) with 0/67 flagged safety engine reports.

Why does Google Play Protect show an alert?

+
Because the APK is distributed directly via GitHub/Developer servers instead of the Google Play Store, Play Protect flags it as an "unknown source". You can safely bypass this by clicking "Install Anyway" or whitelisting the app.

How does offline reading work?

+
When you click "Download" on a subject's handout or past paper, the app downloads and stores the PDF in private local directories. Once downloaded, you can view the PDF offline without an internet connection.

Can I bookmark specific PDF pages?

+
Absolutely. The custom PDF Viewer includes a bookmark overlay. Click the bookmark icon, assign a custom label to that page, and access it instantly through the Room database bookmark tab.
