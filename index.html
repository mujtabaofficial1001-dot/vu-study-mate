Fully Featured Designed for VU Students

Virtual University students ke daily hurdles ko dekhte hue, VU Study Mate app me heavy optimization aur features dale gaye hain, taake aap offline bina kisi battery drain ke parh sakein.

1. My Library & Progress Tracker

A smart, centralized hub to manage and organize all your downloaded handouts and textbooks dynamically offline.

  • Continue Reading Card: Resume the last opened PDF in 1-click.
  • Category Filter Chips: Filter books instantly by course code (CS101, etc.).
  • Precise Reading Stats: Shows active progress bar with page count (e.g. 12/50).

2. Built-In Pro PDF Reader

Read heavy documents smoothly with a custom built-in engine that runs offline with zero context memory leaks.

  • True Night/Dark Mode: Inverts page colors to reduce late-night eye strain.
  • Study Notes & Bookmarks: Save custom formulas or notes to jump back instantly.
  • Auto Page Saving: Automatically remembers your last read page on screen close.

3. Short Video Lectures & Portal

An all-in-one student portal. Seamlessly play your VU short video lectures directly within the app.

  • Integrated Short Lectures: Play YouTube VU lectures inside secure views.
  • Quick Access Shortcuts: 1-click links for **LMS**, **Datesheet**, and **Notices**.
  • History WebView Navigation: Use back buttons without losing active login sessions.

4. Concurrency Download Queue

Download massive textbooks and 10+ reference sheets simultaneously without lagging or crashing your device.

  • 3-Slot FIFO Queue Pooling: Schedules downloads automatically to save battery.
  • Throttled Progress UI: Throttles updates to prevent UI lags and ANRs.
  • Clean Cancel Cleanup: Permanently deletes partial downloads on cancellation.

How to Install VU Study Mate

Since we are sharing this app directly through GitHub for students to use completely free, you must manually install it on your Android phone. Follow these quick steps:

01
Download the APK file

Click the prominent "Download App" button at the top to download the official secure `VU.STUDY.MATE.apk` directly to your phone.

02
Enable Unknown Sources

If prompted by your browser (Chrome/Edge), tap on "Settings" and toggle "Allow from this source" to allow sideloading installation.

03
Click Install & Enjoy

Open the downloaded file, click "Install", enter your program & semester profile details, and start exploring your study files offline!
