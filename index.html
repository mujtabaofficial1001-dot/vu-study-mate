Is this application safe and secure to use?

+
Yes, VU StudyMate is 100% secure. It utilizes local sandbox paths (context.getExternalFilesDir) and only uses internet permissions to sync documents from Firebase storage. The APK has been audited via VirusTotal (SHA-256 verification) with a clean 0/68 security vendor report.

Why does Google Play Protect show an alert?

+
Because the APK is distributed directly via GitHub/Developer releases instead of the Google Play Store, Play Protect flags it as an "unknown source". You can safely tap "Install Anyway" or whitelist the APK.

How does offline reading work?

+
When you tap "Download" on a subject's handout or past paper, the app downloads and caches the PDF in local app storage. Once downloaded, you can view the PDF offline without an active internet connection.

Can I bookmark specific PDF pages?

+
Yes! The custom PDF Viewer includes an interactive bookmark overlay. Click the bookmark icon, assign a custom label to that page, and jump back to it anytime from the bookmark tab.
